A federal law, the first signed by President Obama, that resets the statute of limitations for pay discrimination claims with each discriminatory paycheck. It responded directly to the Supreme Court's Ledbetter v. Goodyear decision, which had let employers escape liability if discrimination began more than 180 days before a claim.
