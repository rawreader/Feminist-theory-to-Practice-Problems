Search engines, facial recognition, hiring tools, and other algorithmic systems that encode and amplify racism and sexism, from degrading search results for Black girls to facial recognition that fails dark-skinned women. Named after Safiya Umoja Noble's analysis of how supposedly neutral technology reproduces oppression.
