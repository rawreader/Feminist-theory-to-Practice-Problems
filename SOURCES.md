# Source verification log

Every link in the seed catalog was checked on June 11, 2026. Two kinds of
verification were possible:

- **Direct (HTTP 200)** — the page loaded and, where ambiguity was possible,
  the page title was checked against the expected project.
- **Bot-blocked but confirmed** — the site refuses automated requests
  (common for government sites and sites behind bot protection), so it
  returned 403/401 to a script, but the organization/law was confirmed real
  and current through independent sources. **These are the ones most worth
  a quick manual click from you.**

## A cautionary tale

clotheslineproject.org — the .org domain — is now a gambling site. The real
project lives at clotheslineproject.info. This is exactly why every link in
this catalog should be clicked by a person before being trusted, and
periodically re-checked.
